# Google Play listing: Gravibloc

Paste these into Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing.

**App name** (30 max)
Gravibloc: Tilt & Pop Puzzle

**Short description** (80 max)
Tilt the board, slide the blocks, pop matching colours. Endless brain teasers!

**Full description**

Tilt the board and every block slides until it hits something. When two blocks
of the same colour touch, they pop! Clear the whole board before you run out of
moves.

Easy to learn, hard to put down:

• Endless levels that get trickier as you go, each one checked to be solvable
• Plan ahead: one tilt moves everything, so think before you swipe
• Earn up to 3 stars by finding the shortest solution
• Stuck? Undo freely, or use a hint to see the next move
• Collect coins and unlock new colour themes: Pastel, Neon and Ocean
• Short levels, perfect for a quick break
• Plays offline, no account needed

Swipe to tilt. Match to pop. Clear the board.
