# Gravibloc privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Gravibloc ("the game") is a puzzle game published by Tenmack. This policy
explains what data the game handles.

## What the game itself stores

Your progress, coins, unlocked themes and sound setting are saved **only on
your device**. The game has no accounts and does not send this information to
us.

## Advertising

The Android version shows ads through **Google AdMob**. To show and measure
ads, Google may collect and process:

- your device's advertising ID,
- your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- device and app information (model, OS version, app version),
- ad interactions such as views and taps.

Google uses this data as described in its own policy:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks
for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice
at any time with **Privacy choices** on the main menu. You can also reset or
delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads or
Settings → Privacy → Ads).

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
data from them.

## Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data. Apart from the ad data handled by Google above,
we do not share any data with third parties.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes all data it saved on your device.
